## Requirement and final-old behavior

v12 supplies three related behaviors that remain in the final released stack:

1. `_check_kvarn_beta_unsupported_config` in `vllm/platforms/xpu.py` admits an
   explicit compact K4V4 draft with a compact K4V2 target, retaining default
   inheritance and matching formats. A K4V4 target with a K4V2 draft remains
   rejected. This is the bounded bundled Qwen MTP1/2 contract, BF16 compute,
   TP1/PP1, eager execution and at most 2,048 batched prefill tokens, with the
   other existing platform guards.
2. `KVarNMetadataBuilder.__init__` in
   `vllm/v1/attention/backends/kvarn_attn.py` derives its group/profile selection
   from `kv_cache_spec.kv_quant_mode.name.lower()`. `_lifecycle_policy` then
   gives compact K4V2 a sixteen-block high-water/eight-block recent-history
   policy and compact K4V4 four/zero. Both tensors use `uint8`, so storage dtype
   cannot distinguish them. Global target cache dtype also cannot determine
   the mixed draft's policy.
3. `_get_kv_cache_groups_uniform_page_size(independent_pools=True)` returns
   whole compatible buckets before equal-layer-count splitting. For sixteen
   target attention layers, one differently formatted draft and 48 recurrent
   layers, this produces group sizes `[16, 1, 48]` instead of 65 singleton
   groups. Matching formats produce `[17, 48]`. The ordinary shared-pool path
   retains its existing split policy.

This preserves supported format options even though the current operational
profile uses K4V2 for both target and draft. The historical workload is
Brutus/Arc Pro B70, W4A16 weights/BF16 compute, bundled MTP2, four scheduler
slots, two-image vision and long context. `xpu-v1.9.0` qualified mixed K4V2
target/K4V4 draft; `xpu-v1.10.0` qualified and selected K4V2 drafting without
retiring the mixed option.

Final-old v15 `2e010408b9713dca3de690f339ef46fbe72f090f` extends admission
to explicit `bfloat16` drafting with a K4V2 target. Its diff preserves v12's
mixed compact case. `auto` and `bf16` remain rejected by this compact MTP
contract. v13 `d6cb2e6cb2de2321fd28eea6e0aada93fb8bd736` repairs reservation
overrides; v14 `32289022a4e362b506a4de1f89e1abe2957660c1` bounds scratch.
Neither removes v12's group-local selection or whole-bucket grouping.

## Allocation, ownership and minimum disposition

**ADAPT; fold into v05 `fcab69494f1719e91e416aace17ef6ad501fe900`.** Preserve
the three behaviors above and their regression coverage; no independent v12
commit or new operator is needed. Upstream covers none of the residual
KVarN behavior, so DROP is not supported.

Tracing the final-old `get_kv_cache_groups`,
`get_kv_cache_config_from_groups`, `_plan_kv_cache_tensors` and
`worker/utils.py:allocate_kv_cache` confirms the relevant invariant. Independent
groups receive separate backing buffers, block counts and local block-ID
namespaces. Layers within a group share the block table while occupying
separate layer-strided bytes. Different compact quant modes/record widths stay
in different compatible buckets. Matching target and draft layers may share a
physical group, but their cache contents and mutable builder ownership do not
alias. The released builder keys state by its exact layer-name set.

Whole buckets remove unnecessary scheduler groups/builders; they do not remove
the per-layer storage costs. Preserve actual page sizes, equal usable attention
token capacity, recurrent reservations for every `max_num_seqs` slot and each
pool's null storage. The original regression compares capacity and total
allocation with the previous grouping for matching and mixed formats. Group
count reduction alone is not evidence of an isolated measured speedup.

Replay the small whole-bucket return on the target helper after its existing
compatibility merge. Enable it only on the KVarN independent-device path,
retaining shared-pool splitting and the early HiSparse/GLM/packed-layout
dispatches. Preserve host placement, roles, transfer flags and target
`num_blocks_of` semantics when v05 integrates its pool fields. Do not replace
the common allocator with an old version that discards target raw-view or
HiSparse handling. No new bounded-draft pool category is requested.
